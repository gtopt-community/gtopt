## Which license do I need?

| Use | License |
|---|---|
| Research, teaching, internal studies, consulting reports | GPL (free) |
| Modifying GTOpt and publishing your changes under the GPL | GPL (free) |
| Running GTOpt with CPLEX, Gurobi, MindOpt or COIN-OR solvers | GPL + Solver Plugin Exception (free) |
| Shipping GTOpt inside a closed-source product, appliance or SaaS you distribute | Commercial |
| Needing warranty, IP indemnity or a support SLA | Commercial and/or support contract |

Licences for proprietary solvers (CPLEX, Gurobi, MindOpt, ...) are **not**
included in either option; obtain them from their vendors.
