# Governance

## Today

gtopt is hosted by the **gtopt community** GitHub organization
(`github.com/gtopt-community`) and led by its author and copyright holder,
**Marcelo Matus**, who acts as lead maintainer:

- every change goes through a pull request reviewed by a maintainer
  (`.github/CODEOWNERS`);
- contributors sign the [CLA](CLA.md) and sign off their commits (DCO);
- the lead maintainer decides the roadmap, releases and licensing
  ([`LICENSING.md`](LICENSING.md));
- regular contributors can be invited as maintainers of specific areas.

Commercial support and services are offered by partners
([`PARTNERS.md`](PARTNERS.md)); partners have no special rights over the
code or the project's decisions.

## Future: a foundation

The project is structured so that stewardship can move to a non-profit
foundation (a Chilean foundation, or an umbrella such as LF Energy or
NumFOCUS) without disruption:

- the CLA licences and the gtopt trademark can be assigned to the
  successor steward, which then takes over the commitment to keep every
  contribution available under an OSI-approved licence;
- the GitHub organization, domain and CI accounts are project-owned, not
  personal, and are transferred with the project;
- existing commercial licences and partner agreements remain valid.

Any such transfer will be announced in advance in this repository.
